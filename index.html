01About me

A brand is a promise kept in every detail.

My path into brand began in nursing, a background that taught me to understand people, build trust, and make decisions grounded in evidence. Curiosity drives how I work, adapting quickly, challenging conventions, and taking calculated risks when they create a stronger brand.

Over the past decade I've worked across healthcare, MedTech, fashion, luxury, entertainment, and founder-led businesses, turning brand strategy into experiences that perform and hold their audience.

My work spans the full brand cycle: strategy and identity, product positioning and launch, campaign development and copy, public relations and events, styling and creative direction. I operate closely with cross-functional teams and agencies, aligning specialists around a single objective and keeping execution consistent from concept to delivery.

As a results-driven professional, my focus relies on sales and performance while protecting the thing that makes a brand worth choosing in the first place: its authenticity.

Experienced across international markets. Fluent in English, Spanish, and Portuguese.

Certified in Brand Management by University of London x London Business School.
